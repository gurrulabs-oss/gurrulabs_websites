Murder mystery riddles 10 short ones, with answers

Ten short murder mystery riddles from the neon side of town, from easy to hard. Each one turns on a single detail. Read it, name the culprit or the liar, then open the answer to check.

Short answer
Murder mystery riddles are whodunits in a few lines: a crime, a few suspects and one detail that gives the culprit away. Everything you need is in the text, so you can solve each one without special knowledge. The answer is under each riddle.

Easy3 riddles

01

The wet shoes

It has rained on District 7 since eight. At ten, a pawnbroker is found dead in his shop. His upstairs neighbour, Mira, says she has not left the building since six, long before the rain; she came down when she heard the shout. Detective Vey notices the trail of wet footprints Mira left across the shop floor. Is Mira telling the truth?

Show the answerHide the answer
Mira is lying. If she had been indoors since before the rain, her shoes would be dry. She was outside tonight.
02

The blackout

From 22:00 to 22:20 the whole block loses power. At 22:10 a jeweller is killed two floors down. Lin says she spent the blackout cooking noodles on her induction hob. Oto says he sat by the window, watching the dark street. Whose alibi cannot be true?

Show the answerHide the answer
Lin’s. An induction hob runs on electricity, and there was none. Her alibi cannot be true.
03

The live stream

A streamer collapses in her studio at 23:15, poisoned. Her co-host says: “I was in the next room. I saw her fall on the stream and ran in.” The chat log shows viewers asking “stream’s down?” from 23:05 onwards. Can his story be true?

Show the answerHide the answer
No. The stream had been offline for ten minutes, so he cannot have watched her fall on it. He was in the room.

Medium4 riddles

04

The harbour photo

Kai’s alibi is a photo he posted at 18:30: him on the harbour wall, the old clock tower behind him. A broker was killed across town at 18:20. Vey zooms in. The tower’s hands show five past four, and that clock has never been wrong. Does the photo prove Kai was at the harbour at 18:20?

Show the answerHide the answer
No. The photo was taken at five past four, hours before the murder, and posted later. At 18:20 nobody knows where Kai was.
05

Two cups

A hacker is found dead at her desk. On the table, two cups of tea: hers is empty, her guest’s is full and still steaming. Her visitor, Ren, says he left more than an hour ago. Is Ren telling the truth?

Show the answerHide the answer
Ren is lying. Tea poured an hour ago would be cold. Her guest was still there minutes ago.
06

The dead sign

A witness, Ona, says she saw the killer’s face at midnight in the alley, lit by the bar’s neon sign. The power company confirms the sign has been dead since a short circuit at 23:00. No other light reaches the alley. Could Ona have seen a face by that sign?

Show the answerHide the answer
No. With the sign dead and no other light, the alley was dark. Ona’s story cannot be true.
07

The wrong wrist

A collector is found with his smashed watch stopped at 21:15. Only two people had a key to his flat: Arlo, who was at a dinner with ten guests from 21:00 to 22:00, and Bea, who has no alibi at all. In every photo the collector wears his watch on the left wrist. Tonight it is on the right. Vey is sure the killer put the watch on him after he died, to fake the time of death. Who is the killer?

Show the answerHide the answer
Arlo. Someone put the watch on him after he died and left it at 21:15. A fake time only helps the one person with an alibi for exactly that time.

Hard3 riddles

08

The silent guard

A shop’s guard robot logs every stranger who comes near the door, the only one there is, and it works perfectly. On the night of the murder its log is empty. The suspects: a courier on his first delivery there, the owner’s nephew who has worked in the shop for years, and a rival who has never been inside. Who did it?

Show the answerHide the answer
The nephew. The robot only logs strangers. An empty log means the killer was someone it already knew.
09

The robot vacuum

The night guard finds a manager dead in his office at 07:00. He says he unlocked the door at 06:55 and spent the night on the ground floor. The doctor puts the death around midnight. The robot vacuum cleaned the whole office at 03:00 and met no obstacle where the body lies. After 02:00, the door log shows only the guard in the building. Who did it?

Show the answerHide the answer
The guard. At 03:00 the body was not there, so it was moved in later. Only the guard was in the building after 02:00.
10

One true sentence

Three suspects, one killer, and only one of them tells the truth. Ash: “Bo did it.” Bo: “Ash is lying.” Cy: “I didn’t do it.” Who is the killer?

Show the answerHide the answer
Cy. Try each suspect as the killer. If it was Bo, Ash and Cy both tell the truth. If it was Ash, Bo and Cy both do. Only if it was Cy is exactly one sentence true: Bo’s.

How do you solve a murder riddle?

Read it twice and look for the detail that seems like scenery: the weather, a light, a time, an object. The riddle is built around it.

Find the hard factRain, a blackout, a clock, a log: something nobody can argue with.
Test each storyHold every statement against that fact. One will not fit.
Forget the motiveEveryone has a reason. The answer is who could have done it, or who lied.

Are these riddles fair?

Yes. Each one gives you every clue in its own text and has a single answer, the way classic detective writers said a fair mystery should (Knox, 1929; Van Dine, 1928).

Every clue is on the pageYou never need outside knowledge, only the text.
One answer onlyIf two answers seem to fit, reread: the clue that separates them is in the text.
