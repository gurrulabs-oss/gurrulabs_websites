What is Cyberpunk Detective? It is a free cyberpunk detective game for Android that mixes detective cases you solve (gather clues, question suspects, accuse the culprit) with audio crime stories you listen to, in a neon-noir world.

How a case plays

Three steps, one culprit.

Search the sceneVisit the locations and examine the evidence. Every clue goes into your clue diary.
Question the suspectsEach interrogation has three rounds of two questions. Pick one per round: the other is gone for good.
AccuseName the killer. The right call closes the case; a wrong one gets its own ending and locks the case for an hour.

Inside the app

Questions

Is Cyberpunk Detective free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play, with ads and no in-app purchases. You don’t need an account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Every case, its art and its audio ship inside the app, so you can play without a connection once it is installed.

What languages is it in?

The game text is in English, Spanish and French. The audio cases are narrated in English, with English captions.

Why does the app say "Mini AI Crimes"?

On phones set to Spanish or French, the app’s icon may read Mini AI Crimes. It is the same game.

How long is a case?

Audio cases run 15 to 25 minutes. Detective cases have no set length: you play at your own pace.
