Questions

Is Cyberpunk Detective free?

Yes. It is free on Google Play and you don’t need an account.

Does it work offline?

Yes. Once installed, you can investigate without a connection.

What languages is it in?

The game is in English, Spanish and French. The narrated classics are voiced in English only.

How long is a case?

A mini case takes 6 to 8 minutes, so it fits a commute or a break. The narrated classics are longer.

Is it a murder mystery game?

Yes. It is a cyberpunk detective game and most cases are whodunits built on deduction, not luck: every clue you need is in the scene and the statements.

Can I play a case without installing?

Yes. Blackout at Hawk Tower runs in your browser and takes about 8 minutes.
